# Matchstick Design Studio — Iteration 2 design notes

This prototype is intentionally built with placeholder copy and placeholder artwork. No placeholder project, client, founder, testimonial, service detail, pricing statement, or process statement should be published as fact.

## Design hierarchy used

The external design skills were reconciled in this order so they do not fight each other:

1. Accessibility and usability are non-negotiable.
2. Apple Design guidance informs clarity, hierarchy, familiar controls, hit areas, focus, restrained materials, and reduced motion—not an imitation of Apple.com.
3. Impeccable guidance is used as the anti-template craft floor: deliberate hierarchy, fewer decorative accessories, no generic card soup, and a clear visual thesis.
4. Taste Skill drives the art direction: image-led studio site, one memorable brand gesture, portfolio-first composition, and avoidance of generic AI-site patterns.
5. Emil Kowalski's animation guidance controls motion: animate only when there is a purpose, use transform/opacity, strong ease-out, hover only on fine pointers, and support reduced motion.

## Visual thesis

A quiet, image-led editorial system on a bright neutral canvas, with black typography and one vermilion "matchstick" accent. The accent is intentionally concentrated in the hero, calls to action, and location moment rather than repeated as decoration.

## Motion thesis

Motion is intentionally sparse. The hero enters once on initial load with a short stagger. Project imagery gets subtle pointer feedback. Service arrows provide directional feedback. FAQ state changes remain direct. There are no scroll-triggered reveal stacks, parallax text, scroll-jacking, cursor gimmicks, or endless loops.
